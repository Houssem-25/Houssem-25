👤 About me

I am a Senior AI Engineer with expertise in 3D/2D perception systems for autonomous vehicles. My research and engineering work focuses on developing robust computer vision and sensor fusion solutions for complex real-world environments.

With a PhD in Machine Learning and Computer Vision, I combine theoretical research with practical implementation to create production-grade AI systems. My work spans depth estimation, 3D object detection, multi-modal fusion, and optimized model deployment.

Currently at Bosch in Stuttgart, I develop advanced perception systems for autonomous driving and ADAS applications, focusing on safety-critical applications and performance optimization.

Houssem Boulahbal, Adrian Voicila, Andrew Comport
arXiv preprint, 2023

A novel transformer-based architecture for predicting spatio-temporal depth from video in a self-supervised manner, enabling more accurate depth estimation with temporal consistency.

Paper

Instance-aware multi-object self-supervision for monocular depth prediction

Houssem Boulahbal, Adrian Voicila, Andrew Comport
IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (IROS)/Robotics and Automation Letters (RAL), 2022

A self-supervised approach that incorporates instance-level object information for improved monocular depth prediction, addressing the challenges of traditional methods in handling moving objects.

Paper

Forecasting of depth and ego-motion with transformers and self-supervision

Houssem Boulahbal, Adrian Voicila, Andrew Comport
IEEE International Conference on Pattern Recognition (ICPR), 2022

A transformer-based approach for forecasting depth maps and ego-motion in future frames using self-supervised learning, enabling predictive perception for autonomous navigation.

Paper

British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC), 2021

An analysis of conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) examining the extent to which these models actually use conditioning information during generation, with implications for controllable synthesis.
